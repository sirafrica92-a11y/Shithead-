What these numbers mean

Capacity rejections count blocked attempts, not individual people or an actual waiting list. Visits include repeat page loads and may include bots. We do not identify unique visitors. Game starts, completions and actions come from successful server operations. Tracking begins only after the analytics feature is deployed; historical data is not backfilled.
